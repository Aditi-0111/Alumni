## 📋 Feature Breakdown

### 🌐 **Network Page** (Main Connection Hub)
**What it does:** Help you find, connect with, and learn about alumni

**Access:** Click "Network" in bottom navigation

**Three tabs:**

#### 1️⃣ **Find Alumni** (Default Tab)
- **Advanced search** for specific alumni
- **Search by**: Name, skills, company, location, batch year, domain
- **Results show**:
  - Alumni name and current role
  - Company and location
  - Graduation batch
  - Field/domain of work
  - Top 3 professional skills

**How to use:**
1. Click "Network" tab
2. Search by name or specific skills (e.g., "Data Science")
3. Use filters to narrow down:
   - By company (Google, Microsoft, etc.)
   - By location (Bengaluru, Mumbai, etc.)
   - By batch (2015-2023)
   - By domain (Software, Product, Finance, etc.)
4. Click on results to view full profiles

---

#### 2️⃣ **Recommended Connections** (Smart Suggestions)
- **Smart AI-powered recommendations** of alumni to connect with
- **Why recommended**: Shows mutual connections and shared interests
- **One-click connecting**

**How it works:**
1. Go to Network → "Recommended Connections"
2. View suggested alumni
3. See:
   - Mutual connections (people you both know)
   - Common interests (shared fields/skills)
   - Why they're recommended
4. Click **"Connect"** to send request
5. Button changes to **"Request Sent"** after you send

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Find mentors in your field
- ✅ Discover professionals with shared interests
- ✅ Build warm connections through mutual contacts
- ✅ Network efficiently

---

#### 3️⃣ **Network Overview** (Understand Your Network)
- **Visual statistics** about alumni connections
- **Analytics** on where alumni work and are located

**Shows:**
- 📊 Total number of connections
- 🔗 How many are 1st degree (direct connections)
- 🔗 How many are 2nd degree (friends of friends)
- ⏳ Pending connection requests
- 🏢 Top companies where alumni work
- 📍 Geographic distribution (cities)
- 📚 Top fields/domains
- 📈 Network growth over time

**Use for:**
- Understand where alumni are concentrated
- Find which companies have the most alumni
- See your network growth progress
- Plan where to focus your connections

---

### **Skill Endorsements** (On Alumni Profiles)
**What it does:** Verify and recognize alumni expertise

**Access:** Visit any alumni profile

**Features:**
- 👍 Endorse skills you recognize in alumni
- ⭐ See how many people endorsed each skill
- 🏷️ Skills organized by type:
  - Technical (React, Python, Java, etc.)
  - Leadership (Team Management, etc.)
  - Domain (Product Management, etc.)
- ✅ Green checkmark shows you've already endorsed

**How to endorse:**
1. Visit an alumni profile
2. Scroll to "Skill Endorsements" section
3. Click thumbs-up icon on any skill
4. See count increase and checkmark appear
5. This helps you validate their expertise

**Why endorse:**
- Helps alumni showcase their true skills
- Creates a record of verified expertise
- Helps other students find specialists
- Shows your recognition of their abilities

## 🎯 Common Use Cases

### Finding a Mentor in a Specific Field
1. Go to **Network** → **Find Alumni**
2. Filter by **Domain** (e.g., "Product Management")
3. Optionally filter by **Company** or **Location**
4. Browse results and click profiles
5. Check their background and experience

### Connecting with Alumni at Your Dream Company
1. Go to **Network** → **Network Overview**
2. Check **Top Companies** section
3. See which alumni work at your target company
4. Go to **Find Alumni** → Filter by **Company**
5. View their profiles and connect

### Getting Professional Recommendations
1. Go to **Network** → **Recommended Connections**
2. Review suggested alumni
3. Check mutual connections (for warm introduction)
4. Click **"Connect"** on interesting profiles
5. Personalize your connection message

### Understanding the Alumni Network
1. Go to **Network** → **Network Overview**
2. Review statistics:
   - Where are alumni concentrated?
   - Which industries/domains are strong?
   - How has network grown?
3. Use insights to plan your connections

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How do alumni see my profile?**
A: When you send a connection request, they can view your profile and decide to accept.

**Q: What should I say when connecting?**
A: Be genuine and specific. Mention why you want to connect (e.g., "I'm interested in Product Management and saw your experience at Microsoft").

**Q: Can I change my recommendation preferences?**
A: Yes, by updating your profile with new skills and interests.

**Q: How are connections recommended?**
A: Based on mutual connections, shared interests, similar fields, and your profile information.

**Q: Is it okay to send multiple connection requests?**
A: Yes, but personalize each one. Generic requests are less likely to be accepted.

**Q: How do skill endorsements work?**
A: You can endorse any skill of an alumni profile. The count increases when multiple people endorse the same skill.

**Q: Can I message alumni before connecting?**
A: Not directly. Send a connection request first, and message after they accept.

**Q: How do I find alumni in a specific company?**
A: Use **Find Alumni** → Filter by **Company** → View all matching results.
